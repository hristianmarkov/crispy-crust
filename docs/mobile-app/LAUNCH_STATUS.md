## Recipe and timing corrections

Both catalogue copies agree. Sandwich bread now incorporates its sugar/oil and uses a loaf-pan bake. High-rye recipes use more prefermented rye and suitable proof/cooling cues. Pancake, waffle, bagel and pizza cues describe the actual food. Oven warm-up overlaps the final proof without extending it or creating invalid scheduling dependencies. Cooling and counter recovery integrate gradual dough-temperature changes, including continued fermentation while cooling. The model uses explicit approximations; it is not a measured kinetic model for every flour, starter, dough or container.
