# Try the current app

Android **0.1.0 (7)** is available through the [internal tester link](https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4700415689322704031). Use the Google account already enrolled in the test. The Play listing may still show the temporary package name until Google reviews it.

1. **Start locally.** Add a starter, choose its mascot or photo, set its weight and storage location, then log a feed. Changing retained starter should update the removed amount. Check the feeding countdown and starter history.
2. **Plan something you want to bake.** Open a recipe, browse Overview / Ingredients / Step by Step, choose a starter and add time away from the kitchen. Review the timeline and optional shopping list before starting.
3. **Follow a bake.** Swipe between steps, mark one complete, check its waiting timer, and add several photos. Finish the bake and find its photos and notes in Journal. Check that records remain after closing and reopening the app, including offline.
4. **Try the coach.** Sign in using the dedicated reviewer account, **hello@leymora.com**, and its existing password. It has complimentary Pro access through 20 November 2027. Ask one baking question from Help. Core baking records stay local unless you separately enable backup. The coach requires internet and confirmation before sending your question.
5. **Check the phone behavior.** Try a timer with the screen locked, approve notifications when requested, and test Google sign-in with the Play testing account. These physical Samsung checks remain separate from the emulator verification.

The reviewer account's password sign-in, RevenueCat registration, server-verified Pro entitlement, 60-question monthly allowance and a completed OpenAI coach response were checked on 20 November. The corrected native subscription flow was exercised in a headless Android emulator.

**Known limit:** Google subscription plans are not active yet. An unavailable-plans message should not remove existing server-verified Pro access. Do not treat a successful coach request as proof that paid purchases or restore have passed testing. SMTP setup remains with the owner. The signed iOS candidate and current remaining administration are tracked in [Launch status](LAUNCH_STATUS.md).

When reporting a problem, include the app build, screen and steps that led to it. Keep passwords and private photos out of public issues.
